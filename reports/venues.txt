INTERLISTED VOLUME PANEL (C6): where thirteen names trade, Toronto against New York.
Daily share volume, January 2014 (Shopify from its 2015 listing) to 10 Nov 2026.
Compustat on WRDS; published as shares of volume, ratios and counts only.

0. WHAT THE TWO VOLUMES MEASURE
   New York: consolidated, every US exchange and off-exchange trade. Compustat's
   figure agrees with CRSP's (consolidated, matched by CUSIP) within 1% on 96.9% of
   32,868 name-days on 12 names, to 2024 (CRSP's last year on file).
   Not checked: ABX (no CRSP share with its current CUSIP).
   Toronto: most likely the Toronto Stock Exchange's own trades only. Compustat's
   figure agrees with the volume the Montreal file prints within 1% on 94.7% of
   1,500 name-days. In September 2025, when TMX began publishing a consolidated
   Canadian figure, the median name's log(Toronto / New York) moved +0.11, against
   0.27 for a 1-in-20 month: no step. Not confirmed by a written definition.
   So Toronto's share below is a LOWER BOUND on Canada's: one Canadian venue against
   every US one. A FALL over time is ambiguous: it can be trading leaving the TSX for
   another Canadian venue, not for New York. A RISE is not: the TSX gained on every US
   venue together. Differences between names in the same year share the same basis.
   One share for one share: Toronto close / New York close has a median of 1.323 across names (the exchange rate);
   every name between 0.9 and 1.7: yes.

2. DAYS ONLY ONE MARKET IS OPEN: the open venue's volume against its own median over
   the 20 sessions before (1.00 = a normal day)
   New York alone   69 days,  891 name-days: median 0.60 (middle half 0.45 to 0.82)
   Toronto alone    58 days,  749 name-days: median 0.34 (middle half 0.23 to 0.50)
   Read: on a US holiday Toronto trades alone, and on a Canadian holiday New York
   does. The ratio says how much of the usual liquidity is still there; part of the
   flow that normally crosses between the two markets does not turn up on the other.

4. NOT MEASURED

   9:30 to 16:00 Eastern, and the daily US figure does not separate extended hours.
   That needs intraday bars; the Interactive Brokers capture (not yet run) can record
   them. Canada's other venues: not on disk (see section 0).
