Every figure here comes from invented data (scripts/demo_synthetic.py).
Two sets of five markets, 16 days each, quotes about as wide as a liquid
Montreal name's. Thresholds were searched on the first set; everything below
is the SECOND set, which played no part in choosing them.

1. THRESHOLDS: CHOSEN ON ONE SET, SCORED ON THE OTHER
   share of flagged, faulty quotes whose cause was called right, averaged over
   the kinds of fault so that no one kind decides it:
                                      first set   second set
   shipped defaults       (2, 0.5, 0.5)     79.6%       87.5%
   best on the first set  (1, 0.3, 0.7)     81.8%       87.5%
   (thresholds are min_others, shared, together; 27 combinations searched)
   On the second set the tuned choice is +0.1 points against the defaults: not enough to change them.
