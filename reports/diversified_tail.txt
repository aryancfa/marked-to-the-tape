2. HAS THE VaR BEEN HONEST? One-day 99% VaR set each evening, against the next
   day's actual marked P&L
   Only days on which every underlying in the book was valued are tested (120 of 127): on the
   others some files had not started or had ended, and the book was a part of itself.
   120 days tested; VaR averaged 15,651 (from 11,339 to 23,603).
   Days the loss went past it: 2, where a correct 99% VaR expects 1.2.
   Those days: 04 Aug 2026 (loss 20,914 against 13,666), 04 Nov 2026 (loss 33,404 against 20,326)
   Kupiec's test: statistic 0.45, p-value 0.50. Not distinguishable from a correct VaR.
   Basel traffic light: GREEN (a correct model shows this many or fewer 88.04% of the time; yellow starts at 95%, red at 99.99%).
   Read with care: 120 days cannot tell a 99% VaR from a 98% one. The VaR here
   prices each evening's book through its delta, gamma and vega, not in full. And a
   window of the 250 days before knows only the market those days had: when moves
   get larger than that year's, a historical VaR is too small until it catches up.
   Over these days the book's actual daily st. dev. was 7,115; the windows behind the VaR implied about 6,729.
