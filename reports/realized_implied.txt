Implied vol against the vol that followed. For each monthly expiry that has
expired inside the data: at-the-money implied vol on the first day of the window,
minus the vol the share realized from that day to expiry. Windows do not overlap.

1. HOW MANY WINDOWS THERE ARE
   15 names, 5 expiries, 75 name-windows with both numbers; 0 where the share price sat outside the strikes with a vol on the first day, so no at-the-money vol was read.
   A name has at most 5 windows. Windows on different names in the same month are NOT independent: one market move reaches
   all of them. The honest count of independent observations is nearer 5 than 75.
