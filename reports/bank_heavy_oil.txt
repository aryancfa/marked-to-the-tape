The WCS discount as a factor, for bank_heavy.csv, a made-up book.
Monthly, 2014-02 to 2026-09 (152 months). MODEL prices on a rule-based vol below.

   and per 1% that WCS RISES against WTI (the discount narrowing). A negative
   'discount' figure means the name tended to do better when the discount WIDENED.

   Names whose discount sensitivity is two standard errors from zero: none.
   No name's sensitivity to the discount is distinguishable from zero. If anything the
   producers (CNQ, SU) did slightly BETTER when it widened: they own
   upgraders and refineries that turn heavy crude into products priced off WTI, so
   cheap heavy oil helps that part of the business. The discount was first measured
   in dollars a barrel, which showed nothing either; the share-of-price measure was
   chosen after both were tried. Read the signs as a hint, not a finding.

   'all fall more' and 'all fall less' move every name's sensitivity two standard
   errors at once, toward a bigger fall or a smaller one; they bound the answer
   loosely. The carry is the same month passing with no move at all, kept apart as
   in the stress report (B6).

Vol: the C1 beta rule on each name's move (no market vol exists for an imagined
month). Oil prices: Government of Alberta economic data, OilPrices, monthly averages.
