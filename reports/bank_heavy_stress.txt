Stress test of bank_heavy.csv, a made-up book. MODEL prices on a rule-based vol:
nothing below is a mark. An instant move, with no day's decay.
Valued on each name's latest day on file (04 Nov to 10 Nov 2026).
The index moves; each name moves by its beta to it. In a FALL the beta is how
the name moved on the index's worst 5% of days since 2014 (scripts/scenarios.crash_betas); in a
rise, its beta over the last 250 days. A calm year's beta understates how names
fall together: CNQ's and Suncor's were negative, which moved them UP in a crash.
