WHERE THE VOL COMES FROM. For names listed in New York, the change in the vol their
New York options showed since the episode's first close (OptionMetrics), at the
position's own maturity AND its own strike: the strike is placed on the smile by
the delta a call there would have, recomputed each day from the moved share price,
and read between the 25-delta put, the money and the 25-delta call (flat beyond).
A skew that steepened moves a low strike more than the money.
The Canadian dollar's part is taken out (src/nyvol.py) and the change is added to the
option's own vol today. Where no New York vol exists (the Toronto funds, a New York
holiday), the C1 beta rule: -0.50 vol points per 1% move, skew 50% steeper past a
-10% fall. No real settlement exists for any episode: Montreal history
on disk starts in May 2026. The WCS-WTI discount is measured apart (the _oil report):
no name's sensitivity to it is distinguishable from zero. The rate move is the bill
curve's, added to today's curve; the funding spread on top is held at today's level.
