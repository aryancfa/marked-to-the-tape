WHERE THE VOL COMES FROM. For names listed in New York, the change in the at-the-
money vol their New York options showed at the position's own maturity since the
episode's first close (OptionMetrics), with the Canadian dollar's part taken out
(src/nyvol.py), added to the option's own vol today. It moves the whole smile in
parallel, so a steepening skew is not captured. Where no New York vol exists (the
Toronto funds, a New York holiday), the C1 beta rule: -0.50 vol points per 1% move, skew
50% steeper past a -10% fall. No real settlement exists for any episode:
Montreal history on disk starts in May 2026. The WCS-WTI oil spread factor is not
built: no price series for it is on disk. The rate move is the bill curve's, added
to today's curve; the funding spread on top of it is held where it is today.
